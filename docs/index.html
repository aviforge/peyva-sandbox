Peyva Sandbox

Learn distributed systems by building one. A wallet where alice sends bob $20, and the money arrives exactly once.

1. What

You build peyva, a small payments system, one piece per chapter. Each piece is there because the chapter before it broke without it.

2. Why

Money is the one kind of data where every distributed systems mistake is visible and costs something. Five rules never break, and every chapter tries to break one.

3. How

How a chapter works: read the What and the Why, copy the prompts into your AI assistant, then run it and watch it fail the way the chapter said it would.

No solutions. On purpose.

Every other course hands you the finished code. Here there is none. The chapter tells you how it should fail, never how to fix it, and the working out is the only part that sticks.
